## Design Context

### Users

The owner confirmed on 23 September 2026 that the website serves Vietnamese beginners and busy people who want guided workouts at home. They need to understand what Vika does, see the app, and find the next step to try it.

### Brand personality

Use encouraging, direct Vietnamese. Avoid a drill-sergeant voice, body shaming, and translated slogans. The owner pointed to Vinafit_mobile for reusable assets. Its authored brand instructions and Vietnamese naming guide establish this tone and provide approved marketing lines.

### Aesthetic direction

The mobile project uses Be Vietnam Pro, ivory #F4EEE2 and #FBF7EE, dark #17120E and #1F1812, and gold #E5B33A. Use dark gold #7F5E10 for text on ivory. These are the starting constraints for website exploration, not an approved website layout. Compare distinct compositions within this identity. Keep the existing logo for the first round.

Use actual app captures to explain the product. Existing lifestyle imagery can help compare layouts, but new artwork and fresh app recordings may replace it once the owner chooses a direction. Do not present generated app interfaces as real screenshots.

### Design principles

1. Show how the product works near the top of the page.
2. Give each section a different job. Remove repeated product explanations.
3. Use concrete Vietnamese copy and existing approved product terminology.
4. Keep text readable on mobile and retain keyboard access and reduced-motion support.
5. Separate established features from proposed features and unsourced claims.

### Open decisions

On 23 September 2026, the owner authorized using the gathered references to redesign the homepage. The implementation uses Cal AI's prominent product screens and feature selector, with an approachable ivory and gold treatment informed by Headspace. It retains experience signup as the conversion and does not imply app-store availability. Reference sites inform visual choices, not product claims.

Later on 23 September 2026, the owner chose Superpower as the main visual reference and Cal AI for the app-native product presentation, and asked for a more creative, premium result than either. The root homepage now follows that direction; the design record is docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-homepage-premium-design.md.
